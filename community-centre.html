ABOUT THE CENTRE

A Space to Connect, Gather & Grow

Haverse Community Centre is a flexible community space located in Senai, Johor, serving the Kulai area. The centre is designed to bring people together and provide a practical venue for a wide range of activities and occasions.

From community programmes and workshops to meetings, gatherings and private events, the space can be arranged according to the needs of your activity.
